Timothy's story

Still in the process of becoming. How mentorship, faith and consistency helped Timothy grow into a web software engineer.

Name
Timothy Olaomoju
Age
22
Today
Web software engineer
Mentor
Mr. Steve

My name is Timothy Olaomoju, and I am 22 years old. My journey so far has been one of learning, growth, faith, and discovering what I can become through consistency, hard work, and the right guidance.

I started my educational journey at NIT, where I was still figuring out my path and what I wanted to do with my life. During this period, I met Mr. Steve at Elevation Church. From our interactions, he showed genuine interest in knowing me beyond what I did. He asked about my education, my family, and my life in general.

Eventually, I joined the artisanship community, and I was fortunate to have Mr. Steve there as someone who could guide and support me. He began sharing valuable lessons about life, personal development, and building things that matter. He also helped me understand the opportunities available to me in tech and encouraged me to explore them.

What stood out to me was that he didn’t just offer advice and move on; he consistently followed up, checked on my progress, and made sure I was taking steps forward. His guidance helped me see possibilities I might not have fully considered and encouraged me to take practical steps toward building a future in technology.

Although I have had other mentorship experiences, Mr. Steve’s consistency in following up and investing in my growth has meant a lot to me. Beyond my professional development, he has also contributed to my spiritual growth, helping me understand the importance of faith, purpose, and having the right values as I navigate life.

Today, I am gradually building something for myself. I work as a web software engineer, collaborating with other people and taking on projects that continue to challenge me and help me grow. Mr. Steve has also entrusted me with some projects, giving me opportunities to put my skills into practice, gain real-world experience, and become more confident in what I do.

Seeing myself grow from where I started to where I am today makes me genuinely happy. I am grateful for the opportunities I have received, the people I have met along the way, and the fact that Mr. Steve is happy to see my progress. It means a lot to know that someone who has invested time and effort in my growth can see me taking steps forward.

I know I still have a long way to go, but I am proud of how far I have come. I am still learning, building, and discovering new possibilities. Most importantly, I am grateful to be part of a community where people genuinely care about one another’s growth and are willing to invest their time, knowledge, and resources in others.

Growth is not just about acquiring skills. It is also about character, faith, consistency, and becoming someone who can create value for others.

Looking back, meeting Mr. Steve has been an important part of my journey. His mentorship has helped me understand that growth is not just about acquiring skills, getting projects, or creating opportunities for myself. It is also about character, faith, consistency, and becoming someone who can create value for others.

At 22, I see myself as someone still in the process of becoming. I am excited about what lies ahead, the things I will build, the people I will work with, and the impact I hope to make. My journey is still unfolding, and I look forward to growing into someone who can also invest in others and make a meaningful difference in their lives, just as Mr. Steve has done in mine.

This is my story so far, and I believe it is only the beginning.
