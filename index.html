Who we are

Helping youth discover purpose

Unleash your gifts. Develop your purpose. Transform your world. Ohrus Global Youth Initiative

Ohrus Global Youth Initiative (OGYI) is a youth-led, faith-friendly, non-governmental and non-denominational skill-building organisation. We empower young people with practical, creative and business skills.

Our mission: to help youth discover purpose through ministry, mentorship and marketable talents. We inspire, equip and empower young people globally through a faith-informed youth ministry, offering life skills, digital education and talent development programs in physical hubs and online spaces.

Who we serve

Young people aged 13 to 25, in urban areas, including:

  • Graduates
  • College and high-school students
  • Young people not in education, employment or training (NEET)
  • At-risk or underserved communities
Inclusion and diversity

We are big on inclusion and diversity. Every young person is welcome at OGYI, whatever their background or beliefs.

How we deliver

Hybrid delivery: local youth hubs together with online workshops, so young people can take part in person or from wherever they are.

Our groups

OGYI young people meet in groups, including Ignited Spirit, Sublime Spirit and our newest group, Living Spirit.

Our impact so far

We measure impact by the young people we serve, the skills and certifications they gain, their feedback and stories, and turnout at our community events.

Safeguarding & child protection

Everyone safe. Everyone valued.

We are committed to a safe, inclusive and nurturing environment for children, young people, adults at risk, staff and volunteers. We have zero tolerance for abuse, exploitation, neglect, bullying, harassment or discrimination.

  • Safer recruitment with references and DBS checks where required
  • Supervised, risk-assessed activities, events and trips
  • Consent first for photos, trips and online sessions
  • Clear reporting to our Designated Safeguarding Lead
Read our safeguarding policy
