Precious's story

My journey with Ohrus Global Youth Initiative. How a new start in the UK became a place to belong, grow in confidence and faith.

Name
Precious Asekhauno
Age
17
Loves
Dancing
Moved
From Nigeria to the UK
Mentor
Mr Steve

My name is Precious Asekhauno and I am a 17 years old girl who loves dancing and expressing myself through my talents. When I first moved from Nigeria to the UK, I wasn’t sure if I would find a group or a place where I could fit in and feel at home.

However, after joining the church, I met Mr Steve, who made me feel very welcome. Him and his wife have become a big part of my life. Mr Steve has become a mentor to me, someone I look up to and feel comfortable talking to about anything.

Since joining the group, I have grown not only as a person but also spiritually. I have learned more about myself, built my confidence, and become more comfortable around others. I am grateful to be part of Ohrus Global Youth Initiative because it has given me a sense of belonging and people who genuinely care about my growth.

Being part of Ohrus Global Youth Initiative has given me a sense of belonging and people who genuinely care about my growth.

Looking into the future, I hope to continue growing, developing my talents, and becoming the best version of myself. I also hope that one day I can become a mentor to younger people, just as Mr Steve has been to me. I want to encourage them, support them, and help them believe in themselves.

I am excited about what the future holds, and I believe that being part of this group is just the beginning of something great. I hope to become someone who makes a positive difference in other people’s lives, just as others have made a difference in mine.
