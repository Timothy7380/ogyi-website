Lolade's story

From a teenage church dance team to leading choreography, teaching dance and mentoring newcomer African youth in Canada.

Name
Lolade
Age
21
Today
Youth Educator & Dance Instructor
Mentor
Mr. Steve

I had the privilege of being mentored by Mr Steve during a season of my life where I was navigating being fresh out of high school, balancing expectations, and attempting to find purpose and community all at once. As one of my teenage church leaders, it was evident that he was truly passionate and committed to seeing me and the other youth grow into thriving individuals. Mr. Steve helped us recognise that we had gifts worth developing and that we were made for more. When we started our dance team, he helped us with coordination, rehearsal support, connecting us to performance opportunities and competitions, and constantly encouraging us to keep growing. There was even a time he graciously travelled with us to a competition more than two hours away :)

His mentorship even extended beyond our talents and activities to character development. As teenagers with different personalities, and opinions, conflicts within our peer group were bound to happen. During those moments, Mr. Steve helped us navigate the disagreements by mediating the conversations, addressing character flaws when necessary, and extending grace as we worked through those difficult situations. His guidance helped us reach resolutions and build better relationships among one another eventually. And as we continued to grow spiritually, he also made himself available to teaching and answering our questions about the faith with much clarity.

Fast forward to when I moved abroad for my undergraduate studies, and Mr. Steve remained gracious enough to check in on me regularly. Even with the distance, he continued to remind me to keep my passion alive and remain committed to developing the gifts God had given me.

Four years later, following my graduation, I can still see the impact of his mentorship in different areas of my life. I have continued to develop my skills, share my gifts, host dance classes, perform at events, lead and teach choreography, and build a community with like-minded dancers. Many of the lessons he shared while guiding our dance team also helped me develop my teamwork, leadership, and coordination skills.

Beyond dance, I have remained steadfast in my faith, joined the worship team, and continued to use my voice more. I have also grown into mentoring and supporting others. His mentorship helped me appreciate the importance of accountability and having the right people to provide guidance as you navigate different seasons of life. Today, I work as a peer mentor to newcomer African youth in Canada and manage a team of peer advisors at my university. It is truly amazing to see how lessons and encouragement I received as a teenager have continued to shape how I show up for others today.

Pursue what God has placed in your heart with audacity and boldness.

To every young person navigating growth, and uncertainties with purpose, my encouragement is to remain courageous. Pursue what God has placed in your heart with audacity and boldness. Be willing to learn, seek accountability, and surround yourself with people who will challenge you to grow. Above all, seek the will of God wholeheartedly and commit your ways to Him, trusting that He will establish your plans (Proverbs 16:3).
